Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Creator Toolkit ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website at creatortoolkits.in.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask you to submit personal details such as your name or phone number to use our tools. When you use our AI tools, your input (topics, keywords) is sent to the AI API to generate results and is not stored on our servers.
