What this tool does

Type your channel's topic, for example "Canva tutorials" or "Telugu home cooking", and get ten video ideas. Each idea comes with a working title and one line on why it works: the problem it solves for a viewer or the search it answers. Ideas cover different formats, such as how-tos, beginner mistakes, comparisons and common questions, so you are not making the same video ten times.

Why idea quality matters more than volume

On a small channel, a few videos usually bring most of the views. When I compared my own ten most-viewed Telugu videos, two of them carried about two thirds of the views, and both answered a specific question people were already searching for. Ideas that solve a clear problem keep getting found long after upload. Generic ideas that could fit any channel rarely do.

How to pick from the list

  • Search check: type the idea into YouTube search. If suggestions appear as you type, people are looking for it.
  • Gap check: look at the top results. Are they old, in another language, or confusing? A clear version in your language can win.
  • Can you show it? Prefer ideas you can demonstrate on screen with your phone or laptop, step by step.
  • Can you finish it this week? Consistency beats one perfect video you never publish.

Turning an idea into a video

Once you pick an idea, refine its title with the Title Generator and write a strong opening line with the Viral Hook Generator. If the idea is small enough, make it a Short first with the Shorts Script Generator. If it gets interest, make the full tutorial next. After publishing, add chapters so viewers can jump to the step they need.

What the AI can and cannot do

The tool knows common patterns of useful YouTube videos, but it does not see your channel analytics or live search data, and it can be wrong about details. Use the list as a brainstorm, not a plan. The best ideas often come from the comments on your own videos, so read those too. The generator is also told not to invent statistics or income claims, so the titles stay honest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get video ideas in Hindi, Tamil or Telugu?

Yes. Choose any of 13 Indian languages, English, or a mix with English.

Are these ideas based on live YouTube data?

No. They come from an AI model that knows common patterns of useful videos. Check each idea in YouTube search before you film it.

Can I generate again for different ideas?

Yes. Each click gives a new set. Try a narrower topic for more specific ideas, such as 'Canva logo design' instead of 'Canva'.

Does it work for Shorts ideas?

Yes. Many ideas can be cut down into a Short. Use the Shorts Script Generator to write it.
