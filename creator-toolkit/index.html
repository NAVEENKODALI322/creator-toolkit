A free YouTube toolkit for creators across India

Creator Toolkit is a free YouTube toolkit, sometimes searched as YT toolkit or YT tool kit: a set of AI tools for Indian YouTubers who run their channels without a team. It drafts the text work every upload needs: the title, the description, the tags, the hashtags, the first line you say on camera, and the script for your next Short.

India watches YouTube in many languages, and most viewers search in a mix. Someone in Hyderabad types "Canva lo logo ela cheyali", someone in Delhi types "Canva se logo kaise banaye", and app names stay in English for both. That is why every AI tool here can write in Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Urdu, Kannada, Odia, Malayalam, Punjabi, Assamese, Bhojpuri or English, and in a natural mix of any of these with English.

The YouTube Title Generator follows patterns that work on YouTube and keeps the main idea within the first 60 characters so it is not cut off on phones. The YouTube Description Generator puts your keyword in the opening lines, and the YouTube Tags Generator and Hashtag Generator suggest tags the way viewers search. The Shorts Script Generator writes a timed 30 to 60 second script, and the Video Ideas Generator suggests ten specific ideas for your niche. Two tools run fully in your browser: the Chapters Formatter fixes timestamps so YouTube shows chapters, and the Title & Description Checker shows length, a phone preview and the hashtag limit before you publish.

AI output is a starting point. Read every suggestion, keep only what is true to your video, and change the wording to sound like you. Titles that promise more than the video delivers hurt watch time, so accuracy matters more than clicks.
