Free AI tools for YouTube creators, built for Telugu channels

Creator Toolkit is a free set of AI tools for YouTubers, especially Telugu and Indian creators who run their channels without a team. It drafts the text work every upload needs: the title, the description, the tags, the hashtags and the first line you say on camera.

Most Telugu viewers search in a mix of Telugu and English. Tool and app names like Canva, Fiverr or Instagram are usually typed in English even when the video is in Telugu. That is why the Title and Description generators offer a Telugu + English mix mode, not only pure Telugu or pure English.

Each tool gives you several options instead of one fixed answer. The Title Generator follows patterns that work on YouTube, such as a specific outcome, a question or a curiosity gap, and keeps the main idea within the first 60 characters so it is not cut off on mobile. The Description Generator puts your main keyword in the opening lines and adds a short summary and call to action. The Tags Generator mixes broad and specific tags, and the Hashtag Generator suggests tags tied to your topic instead of generic ones.

The Viral Hook Generator writes opening lines for the first five seconds, which decide whether a viewer stays. The Shorts Script Generator writes a timed 30 to 60 second script, and the Video Ideas Generator suggests ten specific ideas for your niche. Two tools run fully in your browser: the Chapters Formatter fixes timestamps so YouTube shows chapters, and the Title & Description Checker shows length, a phone preview and the hashtag limit before you publish. The Thumbnail Downloader lets you save a public video's thumbnail so you can study its layout and text for your own designs. Thumbnails belong to their creators, so use them for reference and research, not for re-uploading.

AI output is a starting point. Read every suggestion, keep only what is true to your video, and change the wording to sound like you. Titles that promise more than the video delivers hurt watch time, so accuracy matters more than clicks.
