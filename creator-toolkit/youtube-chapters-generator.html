What are YouTube chapters?

Chapters split a video into named sections on the progress bar. Viewers can see what each part covers and jump straight to the step they need. You create them by writing timestamps in your video description. YouTube reads that list and turns it into chapters, but only when the list follows a few rules. If one rule is broken, YouTube quietly ignores the whole list and no chapters appear.

The rules this tool checks

  • The first timestamp must be 0:00. This tool adds an "Intro" chapter at 0:00 if yours is missing.
  • You need at least three timestamps.
  • Timestamps must be in ascending order. The tool sorts them and removes duplicates.
  • Every chapter must be at least 10 seconds long. If you add the video length, the last chapter is checked too.

Everything runs in your browser. Your timestamps are not sent to any server.

How to use it

While you edit, note the time each new step starts. Paste those notes here, one per line. The tool accepts messy input such as "Setup 1:20" or "[01:20] - Setup", fixes the format, and tells you what is still wrong. Copy the result and paste it into your YouTube description, usually after the first paragraph. Save the video, then refresh the watch page to see the chapters on the progress bar.

Why chapters help a tutorial channel

Most Telugu tutorial videos answer one search, such as "Canva lo logo ela cheyali" or "Fiverr account ela create cheyali". Many viewers arrive already knowing part of the process and want only one step. Without chapters they scrub around, give up and leave. With chapters they jump to the step and stay. That keeps viewers on your video instead of sending them back to search.

Chapters can also appear in Google search results as "key moments", which gives a tutorial an extra way to be found. YouTube can generate automatic chapters for some videos, but your own chapter names are clearer and use the words your viewers search for.

Writing good chapter names

  • Name the action, not the order. "Account create cheyadam" is clearer than "Step 1".
  • Keep each name short so it fits on the progress bar on a phone.
  • Use the same mix of Telugu and English you speak in the video. If you say "template select cheyandi", write that.
  • Do not stuff keywords into chapter names. Viewers read them before deciding to stay.

Chapters not showing? Check these

If the list passes every check here but chapters still do not appear, wait a few minutes and refresh. Make sure each timestamp starts its own line and is not inside a link. Remember that YouTube can turn chapters off for some videos, and they are not shown on every surface, for example in some embedded players. Shorts do not support chapters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are my YouTube chapters not showing?

Usually one rule is broken: the first timestamp is not 0:00, there are fewer than three timestamps, a chapter is shorter than 10 seconds, or the times are out of order. Paste your list here to find which one.

Where do I paste the chapters?

In the video description in YouTube Studio. Each chapter goes on its own line, starting with the timestamp.

Can chapter names be in Telugu?

Yes. Chapter names can be in Telugu script, English, or a mix. Use the words your viewers search for.

Do YouTube Shorts support chapters?

No. Chapters are for regular long-form videos.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser.
