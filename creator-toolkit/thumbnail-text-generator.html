What thumbnail text is for

Thumbnail text is the two to four big words written on your thumbnail. It is not a second title. The title tells people what the video is about. The thumbnail text adds the reason to click: the result, a surprise, a warning or a question. Together they should say more than either says alone. This tool suggests ten short options for your topic in Telugu, English or the Telugu + English mix, so you can pick the one that fits your design.

Rules that keep thumbnail text readable

  • Two to four words. Most people see your thumbnail at about the size of a postage stamp on a phone. Long text becomes a blur.
  • Do not repeat the title. If the title says "Canva logo design", the thumbnail can say "FREE LOGO in 5 min?" only if the video truly shows that, or simply "Like a PRO".
  • One idea per thumbnail. One face, one object, one short line of text is easier to read than three competing elements.
  • High contrast. Thick fonts, a dark outline or a solid box behind the text help it stay readable on any background.

Indian-language text on thumbnails

Indian scripts like Devanagari, Telugu or Tamil have more detail than English letters, so they need a bigger, bolder font to stay readable when small. Many creators mix languages on the thumbnail: a word in their language for emotion ("ऐसे करें", "ఇలా చేయండి", "கவனம்") and an English word for the tool or result ("Canva", "Free Logo"). Choose a Mix option to get that style. Test your thumbnail by shrinking it on your own phone screen. If you cannot read it in one glance, cut words.

Pairing text with your title

Write the title first, then the thumbnail text. If the title explains the topic ("How to design a logo in Canva, Telugu"), the thumbnail can show the result or emotion. If the title is a question, the thumbnail can show the answer. Use the Title & Description Checker to make sure your title is not cut off on phones, and the Thumbnail Downloader to study the layout of thumbnails that work in your niche.

Honest text only

The generator is told not to invent numbers, prices or results, and not to use hype words. A thumbnail that promises something the video does not show gets clicks but loses viewers quickly, and YouTube notices when people leave early. Keep the promise small and true, and deliver it in the first minute.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many words should thumbnail text have?

Two to four words. It has to be readable when the thumbnail is shown very small on a phone.

Should thumbnail text be the same as the title?

No. The title explains the topic. The thumbnail text should add curiosity or show the result.

Can I use Hindi, Telugu or Tamil text on thumbnails?

Yes. Use a large, bold font for your script with strong contrast so the letters stay readable when small.

Does this tool make the thumbnail image?

No. It suggests the text. Design the thumbnail in Canva or any editor.
