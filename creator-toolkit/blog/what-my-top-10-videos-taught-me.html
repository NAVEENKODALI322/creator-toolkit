What My 10 Most-Viewed Videos Taught Me: Fiverr, Canva and Freepik by the Numbers

By Naveen Kodali, Telugu creator and graphic designer

Oct 26, 2026 · 4 min read

My Telugu YouTube channel is about earning online as a designer: Canva, Fiverr and Freepik. I took the ten most-viewed videos on those topics and put their numbers side by side. This post shares the data and what I think it suggests.

All view counts are from YouTube on 26 October 2026. This is one channel and only ten videos, so treat the patterns as hints, not rules.

The ten videos

Video Topic Published Views Avg. views per month
How to create a Fiverr account and earn money Fiverr Feb 2024 46,829 about 1,430
Earn money with Canva: step-by-step guide Canva Jul 2024 39,975 about 1,470
Earn money from Canva without any skills Canva Jul 2024 8,063 about 300
Canva earning guide 2025 Canva Jun 2025 7,730 about 480
Create and sell on Canva: best way to earn? Canva Sep 2025 6,727 about 510
Ordered a graphic tablet with Canva money Canva Mar 2025 6,698 about 350
Earn money with Freepik Contributor Freepik Jul 2024 3,406 about 130
How to create a Fiverr gig from scratch Fiverr May 2024 3,171 about 110
Design PSD files for Freepik with Photopea Freepik Aug 2024 2,855 about 110
Fiverr delivery process: $25 for a one-minute video Fiverr Mar 2025 2,505 about 130

Together these ten videos have about 127,900 views. The last column is simply total views divided by the months since upload. It is a rough average, because most videos get a large share of their views early.

What the numbers show

1. Two videos carry about two thirds of the views. The Fiverr account video and the Canva step-by-step video have 86,804 views between them. That is 67.8% of all ten. The other eight videos share the remaining third.

2. The biggest videos answer the first question a beginner asks. "How do I start on Fiverr?" and "How do I earn with Canva?" are the questions someone asks on day one. My narrower follow-ups sit between 2,500 and 3,400 views: the Fiverr gig, the Fiverr delivery, the Freepik contributor and the Photopea video. I wrote the Fiverr account video up as a post here: How I Created My Fiverr Seller Account and First Gig.

3. Canva has the most views in total, but Fiverr has the most per video.

Topic Videos Total views Views per video
Canva 5 69,193 about 13,800
Fiverr 3 52,505 about 17,500
Freepik 2 6,261 about 3,100

Freepik is the weakest of the three topics in this list. Its two videos are in the lower half, and neither passes 3,500 views.

4. A Telugu title did not clearly beat an English one. Six of the ten titles contain Telugu script, and four are written only in English letters. The Telugu-script group averages about 12,300 views per video and the English group about 13,600. With ten videos that is a tie, not a result.

5. Age matters, so compare carefully. More than 81% of these views come from videos published in 2024, which have had the most time to collect views. Three of the four 2025 videos average 350 to 510 views per month. They are not weak, they just have fewer months behind them.

What I would take from this

If you are starting a similar channel, these are the three things the data points to:

  • Start with the broad beginner video on a topic, then make the narrow follow-ups. My two biggest videos are the broad ones.
  • Count your videos per topic before you decide what is working. Fiverr has only three videos here and still holds 41% of the views.
  • Do not read too much into one result. Ten videos from one channel can show a direction, but not a rule.
