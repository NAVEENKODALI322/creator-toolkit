How My Telugu Canva Video Reached 39,000 Views (Real YouTube Analytics)

By Naveen Kodali, Telugu creator and graphic designer

Oct 5, 2026 · 4 min read

In July 2024 I published a Telugu video called "Earn Money with Canva: A Step-by-Step Guide." I did not expect much from it. Today it has about 39,500 views, and it is one of the best-performing videos on my channel. In this post I am sharing the real YouTube Studio numbers, what I think they mean, and what I would copy if I were starting a Canva channel in Telugu today.

I am Naveen Kodali, a graphic designer from Andhra Pradesh. I also upload elements to the Canva Creator Program, so this topic is close to my own work.

The numbers, straight from YouTube Studio

  • Views: about 39.5K
  • Unique viewers: about 28.1K
  • Thumbnail impressions: about 3.5 lakh (350,000)
  • Thumbnail click-through rate: 9.1%
  • Watch time: about 1.8K hours
  • Subscribers gained from this video: +318
  • Average view duration: 2:45 on a 9:35 video
  • Main traffic source: YouTube search, about 60.7% of views

YouTube also shows this video doing far better than my usual videos, with about 36.4K more views than my typical performance.

Why I think it worked

I cannot see inside YouTube's algorithm, so these are my honest guesses, backed by the numbers above.

1. People were already searching for it. Over 60% of views came from YouTube search. That tells me viewers were typing something like "earn money with Canva" in Telugu and finding this video. I did not need a viral moment. I needed a topic people actively want.

2. The thumbnail did its job. A 9.1% click-through rate on 3.5 lakh impressions means a lot of people who saw the thumbnail chose to click. My thumbnail uses my face, a large "EARN FROM Canva" headline, and a clean background, with minimal text. The idea is simple: one clear promise that is readable even on a small phone screen.

3. The title is plain and specific. The title says exactly what the video is: earning money with Canva, step by step, in Telugu. It does not use clickbait. When someone searches for that, the title matches what they typed.

4. The topic matches a real need. Many Telugu viewers want a way to earn from design without expensive software. Canva is free to start, which makes it easy to try. That is a real reason people keep finding this video more than two years later.

What I tested: the A/B test

In May 2026 I ran YouTube's thumbnail and title A/B test on this video for a week. The result was "no clear winner," so YouTube kept Variant 1 live for everyone.

I like this result because it taught me something: the original packaging was already strong enough that the changes I tried did not beat it. Not every test needs a winner to be useful.

What I would improve

I want to be honest, because the numbers are not perfect.

The average view duration is 2:45 on a 9:35 video, which is about 29%. That means many viewers leave early. If I remade this video today, I would:

  • Show the end result in the first 15 seconds
  • Cut slower sections in the middle
  • Use clearer on-screen steps so viewers can follow without rewinding

Good click-through rate gets people in. Good retention keeps them watching. This video has the first part, and I am still working on the second.

What this means for my Canva work

I upload to the Canva Creator Program and I teach Canva on YouTube. The two support each other. The channel gives me a reason to learn Canva deeply, and the Creator Program gives me real results to talk about. My Canva videos consistently get better CTR and views than my other topics, and this video is the clearest example.

If you want to read the other side, I wrote about my actual royalty numbers in My Canva Creator Program Earnings: Real Numbers From 2025 and 2026.

If you want to start a Canva channel in Telugu

  1. Pick a topic people already search for. Check YouTube's search suggestions first.
  2. Make the title say exactly what the video delivers.
  3. Use one face, one big headline, and very little text in the thumbnail.
  4. Do not judge a video in the first week. This one grew over more than two years.
  5. Watch retention, not just views. Views tell you who clicked. Retention tells you who stayed.

I will keep sharing real numbers as they change. If you have a question about Canva or YouTube for Telugu creators, ask in the comments on my channel, @naveenkodali322.
