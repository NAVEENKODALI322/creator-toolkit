## Why I think it worked

I cannot see inside YouTube's algorithm, so these are my honest guesses, backed by the numbers above.

**1. People were already searching for it.**
Over 60% of views came from YouTube search. That tells me viewers were typing something like "earn money with Canva" in Telugu and finding this video. I did not need a viral moment. I needed a topic people actively want.

**2. The thumbnail did its job.**
A 9.1% click-through rate on 3.5 lakh impressions means a lot of people who saw the thumbnail chose to click. My thumbnail uses my face, a large "EARN FROM Canva" headline, and a clean background, with minimal text. The idea is simple: one clear promise that is readable even on a small phone screen.

**3. The title is plain and specific.**
The title says exactly what the video is: earning money with Canva, step by step, in Telugu. It does not use clickbait. When someone searches for that, the title matches what they typed.

**4. The topic matches a real need.**
Many Telugu viewers want a way to earn from design without expensive software. Canva is free to start, which makes it easy to try. That is a real reason people keep finding this video more than two years later.

## What I tested: the A/B test

In May 2026 I ran YouTube's thumbnail and title A/B test on this video for a week. The result was "no clear winner," so YouTube kept Variant 1 live for everyone.

I like this result because it taught me something: the original packaging was already strong enough that the changes I tried did not beat it. Not every test needs a winner to be useful.

## What I would improve

I want to be honest, because the numbers are not perfect.

The average view duration is 2:45 on a 9:35 video, which is about 29%. That means many viewers leave early. If I remade this video today, I would:

- Show the end result in the first 15 seconds
- Cut slower sections in the middle
- Use clearer on-screen steps so viewers can follow without rewinding

Good click-through rate gets people in. Good retention keeps them watching. This video has the first part, and I am still working on the second.

## If you want to start a Canva channel in Telugu

1. Pick a topic people already search for. Check YouTube's search suggestions first.
2. Make the title say exactly what the video delivers.
3. Use one face, one big headline, and very little text in the thumbnail.
