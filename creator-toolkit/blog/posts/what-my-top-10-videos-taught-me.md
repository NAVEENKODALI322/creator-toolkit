My Telugu YouTube channel is about earning online as a designer: Canva, Fiverr and Freepik. I took the ten most-viewed videos on those topics and put their numbers side by side. This post shares the data and what I think it suggests.

All view counts are from YouTube on 26 October 2026. This is one channel and only ten videos, so treat the patterns as hints, not rules.

## What the numbers show

**1. Two videos carry about two thirds of the views.**
The Fiverr account video and the Canva step-by-step video have 86,804 views between them. That is 67.8% of all ten. The other eight videos share the remaining third.

**2. The biggest videos answer the first question a beginner asks.**
"How do I start on Fiverr?" and "How do I earn with Canva?" are the questions someone asks on day one. My narrower follow-ups sit between 2,500 and 3,400 views: the Fiverr gig, the Fiverr delivery, the Freepik contributor and the Photopea video. I wrote the Fiverr account video up as a post here: [How I Created My Fiverr Seller Account and First Gig](/blog/how-to-create-fiverr-account-and-first-gig.html).

**3. Canva has the most views in total, but Fiverr has the most per video.**

| Topic | Videos | Total views | Views per video |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canva | 5 | 69,193 | about 13,800 |
| Fiverr | 3 | 52,505 | about 17,500 |
| Freepik | 2 | 6,261 | about 3,100 |

Freepik is the weakest of the three topics in this list. Its two videos are in the lower half, and neither passes 3,500 views.

**4. A Telugu title did not clearly beat an English one.**
Six of the ten titles contain Telugu script, and four are written only in English letters. The Telugu-script group averages about 12,300 views per video and the English group about 13,600. With ten videos that is a tie, not a result.

**5. Age matters, so compare carefully.**
More than 81% of these views come from videos published in 2024, which have had the most time to collect views. Three of the four 2025 videos average 350 to 510 views per month. They are not weak, they just have fewer months behind them.

## What I would take from this

If you are starting a similar channel, these are the three things the data points to:

- Start with the broad beginner video on a topic, then make the narrow follow-ups. My two biggest videos are the broad ones.
- Count your videos per topic before you decide what is working. Fiverr has only three videos here and still holds 41% of the views.
- Do not read too much into one result. Ten videos from one channel can show a direction, but not a rule.
