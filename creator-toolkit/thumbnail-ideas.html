How to use these thumbnail ideas

A good thumbnail has one idea, 2 to 4 big words, and one clear face or object. Pick the concept closest to your video, shoot the expression described, and use the three colors as your base palette in Canva or Photoshop.

Quick tips for Telugu thumbnails

Keep Telugu text short and bold. Use a heavy Telugu font, add a dark outline or shadow, and keep text away from the bottom-right corner where YouTube shows the video time. Test your thumbnail at small size on your phone before uploading.

Why the colors and expressions matter

High contrast between text and background makes viewers stop scrolling. A strong expression such as surprise, excitement, or curiosity tells viewers what feeling to expect from the video.
