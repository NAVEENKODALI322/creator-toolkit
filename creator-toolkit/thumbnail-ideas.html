How to make a thumbnail that gets clicks

Use one clear idea, 2 to 4 big words and one strong face or object. Check what already works for your topic in the reference videos, then make yours more simple and more readable on a phone.

Tips for Telugu thumbnails

Pick a heavy Telugu font, keep a dark outline around the text and keep important words away from the bottom-right corner where YouTube shows the video time. Use a face photo with a clear expression that matches the video feeling.
