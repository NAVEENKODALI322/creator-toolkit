What you get

Describe your Short in one line and choose a length. The tool writes a script in four parts: a hook for the first three seconds, timed spoken lines that fit the length you chose, short on-screen captions, and a closing call to action. You can write it in Telugu, English, or the Telugu + English mix most Telugu creators actually speak.

Why a script matters for Shorts

A Short has no time for warming up. Viewers decide in the first second or two whether to keep watching or swipe. Without a script it is easy to start with "Hi friends, ee roju manam..." and lose half the audience before the useful part arrives. A script puts the payoff or the question first, keeps every line short enough to say in one breath, and ends before attention runs out.

How to turn the script into a Short

  • Read it aloud once. Change any word you would not naturally say. Viewers notice when a line sounds read, not spoken.
  • Record line by line. Each timed line is roughly one cut. Short clips are easier to re-record and keep the pace up.
  • Add the on-screen text as large captions. Many people watch Shorts with the sound off.
  • Check the facts. AI can be wrong. If a step or setting does not match what you show on screen, fix the line.

Choosing a length

30 seconds suits a single tip or one quick result. 45 seconds fits a short process with two or three steps. 60 seconds gives room for a mini tutorial, but every extra second has to earn its place. If you cannot fill 60 seconds with useful steps, choose 30 and keep the energy high.

Honest scripts only

The generator is told not to invent numbers, earnings, view counts or results, and not to use hype like "guaranteed" or "overnight". You will be saying these lines as yourself, and a Short that promises more than it shows gets swiped away, which hurts how far YouTube pushes your next one. If your topic includes a real number, such as "3 Canva tips", the script can use it.
