Why replying to comments matters

On a small channel, every comment is a person who cared enough to type. Replying turns a one-time viewer into someone who comes back, and the conversation under a video shows new visitors that the channel is alive. Comments are also where your best video ideas come from: the questions people ask are the searches they will type next. The hard part is time. This tool drafts three replies for any comment so you can answer more viewers in less time, in the language they wrote in.

Reply styles

  • Friendly: a warm, short reply for general comments like "super anna" or "nice video".
  • Say thanks: for viewers who say the video helped them.
  • Answer a question: simple steps for "how do I..." comments. If the comment does not have enough detail, the reply asks one short follow-up question instead of guessing.
  • Reply calmly to criticism: acknowledges fair points without arguing. Calm replies often turn critics into regular viewers, and other people reading the thread notice.

Replies in your viewers' language

Indian viewers often comment in a mix of their language and English, often typed in English letters ("bhai ye kaise kare", "anna ela cheyali", "bro epdi pannanum"). Replying in the same style feels natural. Pick the viewer's language with or without the English mix, or plain English. Always read the reply before posting and change any word you would not say yourself.

Good habits for comments

  • Reply in the first few hours after publishing, when most comments arrive.
  • Pin a helpful comment, such as an answer to the most common question.
  • Save repeat questions. If three people ask the same thing, that is your next video. Put it into the Video Ideas Generator or make a quick answer with the Shorts Script Generator.
  • Ignore spam and abuse. Do not reply to scam links or insults. Use YouTube Studio to hide or report them.

Privacy

The comment you paste is sent to an AI service to write the replies and is not saved on our servers. Do not paste personal details such as phone numbers or email addresses from a comment.

Can I post the reply as it is?

Read it first and change any word you would not say. The replies are drafts that should sound like you.

What if the comment is rude?

Choose 'Reply calmly to criticism' for fair criticism. For abuse or spam, it is usually better not to reply and to hide or report the comment in YouTube Studio.
